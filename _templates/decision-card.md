# Decision card: the shape every card has

```
/// {AGENT-INITIATED?} · {intent group} · {confidence}% · {customers} customers · {vip} VIP · lift {lift}x
{title}
{situation}

Proposed
  1. {action type}: {params}       {policy note, if the gate changed it}
  2. ...
Replies  [EN] [AR] [{other}]        editable
Evidence  {3 representative messages, original language}   (expand)

[Approve]   [Edit]   [Not this ▾]   [Snooze]
```

A question card replaces "Proposed" with `{question}` + one button per option.
