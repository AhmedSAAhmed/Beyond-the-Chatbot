# Brand voice

- Short: two or three sentences. Customers read on a phone.
- Own the problem first, then the fix, then what happens next. No "we apologise for any inconvenience".
- Name the concrete thing: the order, the refund amount, the date.
- English: plain and warm. No exclamation marks in apologies.
- Arabic: write natural Gulf-friendly Modern Standard Arabic, the way a Saudi or Bahraini support agent writes on WhatsApp: warm, short, respectful. Not a literal translation of the English.
- Reply in the customer's language. If they wrote in French or Spanish, the reply for them is in that language.
- Never promise something the action on the card does not do.
- Never ask for passwords, card numbers or full account numbers.
